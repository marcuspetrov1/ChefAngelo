About · Chef Angelo

Food is culture

I am Angelo, born in Naples and cooking for you in Los Angeles.

Food philosophy

How I think about food

Food is culture. Every dish carries the place it comes from: its land, its sea, its grandmothers. When I cook, I am sharing that story with you.

Events come first as social events. A meal is how people gather. I design every experience so guests talk, laugh and cook or eat together, and the food brings them closer.

Italy, region by region. My menus focus exclusively on Italy’s regions. A Calabrian dish is Calabrian, a Tuscan dish is Tuscan, and I will tell you where each one is from.

No fake authenticity. I do not sell a costume. There is no red-checkered tablecloth act, only honest technique, good ingredients and the dishes as they are really cooked in Italy.

My story

From Naples to Los Angeles

I was born in Naples, and food was the center of everything growing up. I trained at the culinary institute in Benevento, then spent 20 years working as a chef and executive chef in Italy, including at Bocca di Lupo in London.

Along the way I found I loved teaching as much as cooking. I have taught culinary arts at UMass Boston, UMass Amherst and the University of Denver, and I co-taught study-abroad courses in Siena and Rome.

Today I cook for families, friends and teams in homes across Southern California, and I teach classes where you learn the why behind every step.
