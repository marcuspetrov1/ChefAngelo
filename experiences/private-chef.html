From Chef Angelo

Private Chef

There is nothing like eating Italian food that was cooked twenty feet from your table. When you book me as your private chef, I plan the menu with you, shop, bring everything I need and cook it fresh on-site, so the food comes out the way it should.

Birthdays, anniversaries, a quiet Tuesday you want to make special: any event at home is a good reason. I cook the regional Italian dishes I love, and I am happy to build around what you and your guests enjoy.

What to expect

Any at-home event

Dinner parties, celebrations or a family meal. If it happens in your home, I can cook for it.

3 to 10 courses

You choose how long the evening runs, from a few courses to a full ten.

Menu co-planned

We build the menu together around your tastes, your guests and the season.

Cooked fresh on-site

Everything is finished in your kitchen, so it reaches the table at its best.

Sit-down or buffet

Served course by course at the table, or set out buffet style.

Full cleanup included

I leave your kitchen clean when I go.

Pricing

What it costs

From $80/guest. Most dinners average about $90/guest, and the range is $80 to $180 depending on the menu. Seafood is about $150/guest at market price. Gratuity is not included.
