Why Chef Angelo

Food is culture

I cook regionally and honestly, and I'd rather tell you where a dish comes from than dress it up.

  • Rooted in Naples

    My cooking starts in Naples, with the flavors and the habits of a table where everyone stays a little too long.

  • Trained at Bocca di Lupo

    I worked the line at Bocca di Lupo, a kitchen devoted to the regional food of Italy, and I never stopped thinking that way.

  • 15+ years teaching

    I've taught for more than fifteen years. I like a class where you leave able to do it again on Tuesday night.
