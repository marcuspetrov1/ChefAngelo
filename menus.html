Holiday parties

A holiday menu, planned together

Family gathering or corporate party, the holiday table is one of my favorite things to cook for.

I do not hand you a fixed holiday package. We co-plan a regional menu around the people at your table: where your family is from, what your team loves, who does not eat what. Then I cook it fresh in your home.

Tell me about your party and we will build the menu from there.
