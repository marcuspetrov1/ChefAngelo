Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do cooking classes work?

My classes run about four hours and are fully hands-on. You cook, I teach the reasons behind each step, and you sit down to a full meal of everything you made. Choose a theme such as fresh pasta, desserts, meats or seafood, or a regional three-course dinner. Classes start from $110/person, and gratuity is not included.

How do in-home parties work?

We plan the menu together, then I shop, bring everything I need and cook fresh in your kitchen. I can serve a sit-down dinner or a buffet, for any headcount your home can host, and I leave the kitchen clean.

Can I choose the menu?

Yes. Menus are co-planned around your guests, your tastes and the season, and I cook from one Italian region at a time. You can see sample menus on the Menus page.

Do you provide wine? Is it BYOB?

Private chef dinners are BYOB. Bring the wine you like and I will send pairing suggestions for your menu.

What does it cost?

Private chef dinners start from $80/guest, most average about $90/guest, and the range is $80 to $180 depending on the menu. Seafood is about $150/guest at market price. Classes start from $110/person. Gratuity is not included. For parties and other events, send a quote request and I will reply with a quote.

Can I give an experience as a gift?

Yes. You can give a dinner or a class as a gift through Gift an Experience on the Book page, and a class makes a great graduation gift.
